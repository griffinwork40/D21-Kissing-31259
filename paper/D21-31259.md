## 1. Statement

The kissing number tau_n is the maximum number of non-overlapping unit spheres that can simultaneously touch a central unit sphere in R^n. This is equivalent to finding the maximum number of unit vectors in R^n with pairwise angular separation at least 60 degrees (cosine at most 1/2).

We exhibit a kissing configuration in R^21 with **31259 points**, improving the previous best known lower bound of 30779 due to Kevin Li (2026).

> **Credit.** The entire base of this construction is the work of Kevin Li
> (paper.pdf, "Improved kissing numbers in nineteen and twenty-one dimensions",
> github.com/Felpix-Studios/D19-D21-Kissing). We extend his configuration by
> retaining his 27707 sparse base points, replacing all 3072 dense directions
> by re-weighted versions with the same sign patterns, and adding 480 points
> (30 additional orbits of size 16) using joint weight re-optimization and greedy orbit insertion. We also credit H. Cohn and A. Li
> (arXiv:2411.04916) for the foundational 27720-point base and for the
> theoretical framework (Lemmas 2.1, 2.2, 4.1, 4.2 and Proposition 4.3 in Li's
> paper remain the logical core; this work adds new search results on top of
> their proven structure).
> We thank Takhanov and Yun for their broader work on the kissing problem.

## 2. Construction

### 2.1 Starting point: Kevin Li's 30779-point configuration

The base B' contains 27707 points in R^21 (Li's construction, retained exactly):

- **840 roots:** all vectors ±2e_i ± 2e_j for 1 <= i < j <= 21, squared norm 8.
- **26867 octad vectors (210 × 128 = 26880, minus 13):** odd-sign {±1} vectors on each weight-8 word of the binary Golay code G restricted to coordinates 1-21, minus 13 specific deleted patterns on the special octad Q = {3,6,10,11,16,18,20,21}. The 13 deleted patterns are listed in Li's paper (Section 4.3) and in `constructions/D21-31259.txt`.

Li's 3072 new points arise from 192 orbit representatives under the size-16 sign group. Their sign words meet every coset of the [21,9,6] subcode C: 512 words on each of cosets 1393, 1993, 2139, 2787 and 256 on each of 0, 696, 3370, 3986 (Li, paper.pdf, Section 4.3). Our 480 new words come from the 1024 previously unused words. The original representatives have entries of absolute value between ~1.5e8 and ~2.5e8 and squared norms near 1e18. In this work their sign patterns are kept but their weights (coordinate magnitudes) are jointly re-optimized, so every one of these 3072 directions differs from Li's originals.

This gives Li's total of 27707 + 3072 = 30779.

The sign group is generated by words 92361, 20689, 346115 and w = 14723; the support of w is {1,2,8,9,12,13,14}, as in Li's paper.pdf, Section 4.3.

The exact vectors are the certificate and are listed in data/; this text explains how they were found and is not sufficient to rebuild them without the data.

### 2.2 Additional points found by this work

Starting from Li's exact integer configuration, we ran a **greedy orbit insertion** procedure:

1. **Initialization:** Load Li's 30779 configuration with exact integer vectors.
2. **Joint re-optimization:** Tune the weights (real-valued scale factors applied to each of Li's 192 representative directions) and simultaneously the weights of a candidate new orbit direction from the 1024 previously-unused words of D (cosets 0, 696, 3370, 3986 contributed unused words). The objective is minimax cosine below 1/2.
3. **Integer rounding:** Round the optimized weights to integers at scale ~1e9 (squared norms near 1e18).
4. **Exact verification:** Run the frozen C checker on the candidate. If VALID, save as a new HIT file and add this orbit to the configuration.
5. **Iterate:** Repeat steps 2-4 with the expanded configuration, selecting the next candidate orbit.

This greedy process added **30 orbits of size 16 = 480 new points**, reaching 31259.

The construction uses no coordinates or groups beyond Li's framework. All 27707 base points are retained exactly. The sign patterns of Li's 192 orbit representatives are kept but their weights are jointly re-optimized, so those 3072 directions differ from Li's originals. The additional new points arise from 30 previously-unused words of D, each producing a 16-point orbit under Sigma.

**Total:** 27707 + 3552 = 31259 points (Li's 192 orbits + our 30 additional orbits = 222 orbits of new points).

## 6. Ceiling

With all **27720 base points kept**, any configuration of the family consisting of that base plus weighted sign vectors indexed by words of D has at most **27720 + 4096 = 31816 points**. Exact certificates prove that flat added points are capped at 2048 (reproducing Gonzalez), and that every admissible nonnegative unit weight vector has coordinate sum **> 3.97**. The latter puts each weight vector within 30 degrees of the all-ones direction, so two points on the same sign word would have cosine > 1/2: each word carries at most one point. The theorem does **not** cover configurations with base deletions, including Li's configuration and ours. Attainability of 31816 remains open.

The source certificates are in campaign lane `lanes/C1_ceiling/`: `THEOREM.md`, `CERT/check_flat.py`, `CERT/check_q1.py`, `CERT/q1_orbit_cert.jsonl` and `CERT/perms.json`. Copies are bundled in `ceiling/`. Run `python3 check_flat.py` and `python3 check_q1.py` from that directory. Use the default Q1 verification, not its optional `--self-test`, whose known transcription blemish is disclosed in `ceiling/THEOREM.md`.

## 7. Open questions

This construction keeps Li's base points and sign-group structure, re-optimizes the weights of his 192 orbit representatives, and adds points greedily. Many open questions remain:

- Can more of the 1024 originally-unused words of D be incorporated? The greedy search added 30 of 64 unused orbit classes; others resist insertion.
- Can the 13 base deletions be reduced further?
- What is the true kissing number in R^21?
- The construction was found by local numerical optimization; no global certificate (LP/SOCP bound) certifies that no additional points from the unused words can be inserted.
