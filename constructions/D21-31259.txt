The lower bound for the kissing number in 21 dimensions is 31259.

This construction extends Kevin Li's 30779-point configuration (github.com/Felpix-Studios/D19-D21-Kissing,
commit 9bb343f, paper.pdf, "Improved kissing numbers in nineteen and twenty-one dimensions").
All credit for the base construction belongs to Kevin Li.

Base B' (27707 points, retained exactly from Li):
  840 roots ±2e_i ± 2e_j (i < j, squared norm 8), and 26867 octad vectors:
  210 × 128 = 26880 odd-sign vectors on the 210 weight-8 words of the extended binary
  Golay code G restricted to coordinates 1-21, minus 13 deleted odd patterns on
  Q = {3,6,10,11,16,18,20,21}:
    +--+---+, +--+--+-, +-+--+--, +-+--+++, +-++-+-+,
    +-++-++-, ++--++-+, ++--+++-, ++-+++--, ++-+++++,
    +++-+-+-, +++++---, +++++-++
  (115 odd patterns on Q retained.)

Sign group (order 16):
  Generators of Sigma = span of words (as sets of 1-indexed coordinates):
    g1 = {1,4,7,8,12,14,15,17}  (word 92361 in 21-bit binary)
    g2 = {1,5,7,8,13,15}        (word 20689)
    g3 = {1,2,12,15,17,19}      (word 346115)
    w  = {1,2,8,9,12,13,14}      (word 14723)
  All generators miss Q, so every sign change S_h fixes the 13 deleted points.
  Sigma_0 = span(g1,g2,g3) is a subgroup of order 8 inside C (the [21,9,6] subcode of D).

New points derived from Li's 192 orbit representatives (3072 points):
  Li's 192 sign patterns (words of D) are kept; their weights (coordinate magnitudes)
  are jointly re-optimized, so these vectors differ from Li's originals.
  New points = { S_h(r_i) : h in Sigma, i = 1..192 }, yielding 3072 vectors.
  Li's 3072 sign words meet every coset of C: 512 words on each of cosets
  1393, 1993, 2139, 2787 and 256 on each of 0, 696, 3370, 3986 (paper.pdf, Section 4.3).
  All 3072 of Li's dense directions are replaced by re-weighted versions with the
  same sign patterns; no original dense direction is unchanged.

Additional new points (found by this work, N - 30779 = 480 points = 30 extra orbits of 16):
  Starting from Li's 30779 configuration, joint numerical re-optimization of all 192 existing
  orbit weights plus greedy sequential addition of orbits from the 1024 previously unused words
  of D (the four cosets 0, 696, 3370, 3986 each contributed 256 unused words in Li's construction).
  Greedy orbit insertion: at each step, optimize the weights of all already-included orbits
  jointly with those of one candidate new orbit (16-point orbit of Sigma), accept the orbit if
  exact integer rounding yields a VALID configuration under the frozen checker, and repeat.
  30 additional orbits were successfully added, giving 222 orbits x 16 = 3552 new points total.
  Integer scale approximately 1e9 (squared norms near 1e18, entries |v_i| in [1e8, 2.5e8]).

Total: 27707 + 3552 = 31259 points.

The exact vectors are the certificate and are listed in data/; this text explains how they were found and is not sufficient to rebuild them without the data.

Verification:
  * Frozen C checker: VALID on Mac arm64 and Linux x86_64.
  * Li's check_pairs.py: 488,546,911 pairs, 0 violations, CHECK PASS.
  * Standard-library scripts/check.py: VALID in about 6.5 minutes.
  * Independent freshly written checker (lane V1): VALID in 1.6 seconds.
